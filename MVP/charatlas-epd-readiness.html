Is your biochar ready for an Environmental Product Declaration?

Before you commission an LCA, find out exactly which data you already have and which gaps would stall the process. Aligned to the ISO 14025 & EN 15804+A2 module structure. This tool scores readiness and builds a consultant-ready gap report — it does not create or verify an EPD.

Important limitation

This is an indicative self-assessment of EPD data readiness. It does not perform a full LCA, issue a verified EPD, replace a programme operator, or replace independent third-party verification. EPD/LCA product claims are kept separate from carbon-removal or permanence claims by design.
