Footprint view counts only avoided emissions and fertilizer savings. It is the number you can put on a product. Net view also subtracts stored carbon; it is useful for tracking progress to net zero, but it is not a product carbon footprint.

CO₂e means carbon dioxide equivalent — every greenhouse gas expressed as the amount of carbon dioxide that would warm the planet by the same amount.

The four “what happens today” shares should add to 100%. If they don't, the calculator still adds them straight through, so a row totalling 200% will double-count. Avoided emissions are counted on the whole residue pile whatever share you send to the kiln, because the comparison is against a world where you built no kiln at all.
